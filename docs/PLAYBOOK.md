# Playbook: cheap recipes and making it pay

## Cheapest ways to get each result

| Goal | Recipe | Approx. cost |
|---|---|---|
| Test an idea for free | Short Maker, **Cheapest** tier (Pollinations images, no voice key needed for a silent cut) or paste your own script | $0 |
| Faceless narrated short | Short Maker · Slideshow · Budget · 6 shots (FLUX schnell + Kokoro voice + captions) | ~$0.03 |
| Same, with a recurring mascot | Add a **character** image → keyframes use Seedream edit (~$0.03 each) | ~$0.20 |
| Short with real motion | Short Maker · Animated · Budget (LTX-Video) → review storyboard → approve | ~$0.15–0.30 |
| Better motion for a hero shot | Video tab: draft on Budget, approve a **Premium** final only for the winner | draft ~$0.02–0.25, final ~$0.35–1.20 |
| Meme / thumbnail images | Image · Budget · 4 at once | ~$0.012 |
| Character swap / parody of your own clip | Character Swap · Budget (Wan Animate 480p draft) → approve 720p | ~$0.20 + ~$0.40 for 5 s |

Rules of thumb:

- **Draft cheap, upgrade winners.** Most of the cost is video seconds. Generate many cheap drafts, publish-test, and spend premium money only on what works.
- **Slideshow first.** Ken Burns over good keyframes + strong narration + punchy captions performs surprisingly well and costs cents.
- **Fix one shot, not the job.** Use *Redo this shot* on the storyboard instead of regenerating everything.
- **Fewer, longer lines.** Voice is billed per character; captions read better with short lines anyway.

## Content that tends to work for faceless channels

- Absurd premises told straight: "A pigeon who runs a hedge fund", "If dogs had LinkedIn".
- Recurring characters (use the same `--character` image) so viewers recognise the series.
- History/science micro-stories with a twist at the end.
- "POV" and "Day in the life of …" formats with a consistent visual style.
- Hook in the first second; payoff in the last shot (the script prompt already enforces this).

## Turning it into income (realistic notes)

- **Platform creator programs** (YouTube Partner Program, TikTok Creator Rewards, Facebook/Instagram bonuses) pay on views once you meet their thresholds. They require original, non-repetitive content; mass-produced near-duplicates get demonetised. Vary scripts and add real ideas.
- **Client work:** short promo videos, product shots and ad variations for small businesses. Use the Image tab with product photos as references and Short Maker with your own script. Price per deliverable, not per generation.
- **Selling assets:** thumbnails, stickers, and stock-style clips — check each model's license for commercial use first.
- **Affiliate shorts:** a narrated short about a product category with a link in bio. Disclose affiliate links.

## Rules that keep accounts safe

- **Label AI content** where platforms ask (YouTube "altered or synthetic", TikTok AI-generated label, Meta "AI info").
- **No real people without consent** — especially in Character Swap. No impersonation, no fake endorsements.
- **No copyrighted characters, logos, or music** you don't have rights to. Use your own music or royalty-free tracks for `--music`.
- Keep `MW_DAILY_BUDGET` low while experimenting; raise it only for a planned batch.
